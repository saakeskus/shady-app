# Web app setup

## Android

1. Open the web app link in Chrome while connected to the internet.
2. Open Chrome's **⋮ menu** and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
3. Confirm, then launch the app from its new Home Screen icon.

## iPhone

1. Open the web app link in Safari while connected to the internet.
2. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.
3. Leave **Open as Web App** enabled if shown, then tap **Add**.
4. Launch the app from its new Home Screen icon.

## First-time setup

1. Keep the installed app connected to the internet for its first launch.
2. Open **Menu**, tap the version line, and open **Asetukset**.
3. Wait for **Sovellus on valmis avattavaksi myös ilman verkkoyhteyttä.**
   This means the app is ready to reopen offline.
4. Enter your details and tap **Valitse kuva** to choose a photo.
5. Tap **Tallenna**.
6. Close the app, enable airplane mode, and reopen it to check offline use.
7. Use the Home Screen icon whenever you open the app.

Set up your details inside the installed app, since it can have separate
storage from the ordinary browser tab.

Avoid clearing the app's website data, which can remove saved details and the
offline copy. If the offline copy is removed, reopen the app while online.
